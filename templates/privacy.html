YouTube Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

YouTube Manager is a web application designed to help users monitor YouTube channels, view channel and video statistics, receive channel notifications, and use AI-powered tools for content analysis and idea generation.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we may receive:

  • Your Google account identifier
  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Your profile picture

We use this information to create and identify your YouTube Manager account.

2. YouTube Data

Users can add YouTube channels by Channel ID. YouTube Manager uses the YouTube Data API to retrieve publicly available channel and video information.

This may include:

  • Channel name, description and thumbnail
  • Subscriber, view and video counts
  • Public video titles and descriptions
  • Video thumbnails and publication dates
  • Public view, like and comment counts

YouTube Manager does not request access to modify, upload, delete, or privately manage content on your YouTube account.

3. Information Stored by YouTube Manager

We may store account information, channels added by the user, channel statistics snapshots and application notifications required to provide the service.

4. AI Features

YouTube Manager provides AI-powered features such as channel analysis, content ideas and an AI assistant.

Relevant channel statistics, public video information, and text provided to AI features may be sent to our AI service provider, Groq, to generate responses.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Display YouTube channel statistics
  • Provide analytics and notifications
  • Generate AI-assisted analysis and content ideas
  • Operate and improve YouTube Manager

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, including Google/YouTube, our hosting infrastructure, and our AI service provider.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Application data may be retained while it is needed to provide YouTube Manager. Users may request deletion of their stored account data by contacting us using the contact information below.

8. Google API Services

YouTube Manager's use of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as YouTube Manager evolves. The latest version will be available on this page.
